How it works

  1. Paste a YouTube or Instagram video link above.
  2. Our small server asks yt-dlp for the video's direct download links (a few KB of work).
  3. Your browser downloads the video directly from YouTube / Instagram at full speed. The video never passes through our server, so our connection stays free.

Public videos only. Private, deleted or age-restricted content may fail — there is no login support. YouTube and Instagram do not officially allow downloading; use this for your own videos and content you have the right to keep. Links are temporary.
